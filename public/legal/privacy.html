3. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • create your account and log you in with one-time codes;
  • run surveys, save your drafts and confirm your entries;
  • enforce our rules, including one entry per survey per email and phone and the $999 winning limit in any 12 months;
  • pick winners at random, notify them, process claims and ship prizes;
  • send new-survey alerts, only if you opted in;
  • answer your questions and support requests;
  • produce survey results for our clients, in combined form or without your contact details;
  • keep the Service secure, prevent fraud, and meet legal and sweepstakes record-keeping duties.

We do not use your information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. The only automated choice is the random draw, which treats every eligible entry equally.

5. Texts, emails and your choices

  • Account messages (log-in codes, entry confirmations, winner notices, reminders and shipping updates) go to everyone with an account, because the Service cannot work without them.
  • New-survey alerts go only to people who ticked the alerts box. Turn them off any time in Settings, by replying STOP to a text, or with the unsubscribe link in any alert email.
  • Text message opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for their marketing. Mobile numbers are shared only with our texting provider to deliver our own messages.
  • Language. We send messages in English or Spanish, based on the language you chose in the app.

6. Cookies and similar technology

We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics services. We use only:

  • a log-in cookie that keeps you signed in, for up to [60] days or until you sign out;
  • settings saved in your browser for your language choice and which messages you have read;

8. How we protect it

  • All connections to the app are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • There are no passwords to steal: you log in with one-time codes that expire after 10 minutes and lock after 5 wrong tries.
  • Log-in codes and sessions are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Only authorized staff can see participant details, using a password plus a code sent to their email. Every staff sign-in, draw, export and change is logged.
  • Survey clients never receive your name, email or phone number.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires.

Wherever you live in the U.S., you can:

  • Turn alerts off in Settings, by replying STOP, or by unsubscribing;
  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing [privacy email].

Residents of some states, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia and others with consumer privacy laws, may have additional rights, such as to know what we collect, to correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

We will confirm your identity before acting on a request, usually with a code sent to the email or phone on your account, and respond within 45 days. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of their authority. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision, and we will answer within 60 days.
