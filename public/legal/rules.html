Additional entries, and entries made with more than one account, will be disqualified. Mechanically reproduced mail-in entries, and entries that are illegible, incomplete, late or lost, are void.

If there is a dispute about who made an online entry, it is deemed made by the authorized holder of the email address on the account.

5. Winning limit

No person may win prizes with a combined ARV of more than $999 in any 12 consecutive months, counting every Golden Opinions Draw. Each prize counts at its ARV on its own drawing date. Forfeited prizes do not count.

If a prize would take a person over this limit, that person's entry is skipped in that drawing and another eligible entry is selected. The person may keep entering and becomes eligible again once older wins fall outside the 12-month window.
